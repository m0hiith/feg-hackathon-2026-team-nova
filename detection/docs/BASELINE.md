# BASELINE - Session Quality & Session-to-Action Conversion

_Generated by `scripts/profile.py`. Source: `top_casino_users_event_logs.csv` (332,119 events · 89 players · 2026-08-01 → 2026-08-31 · brand `hr`)._

> Every number below is from the **native** `(PlayerID, session)` key. The 30-minute reconstruction is reported as a cross-check only.


## 1. Is there a real session id? — YES

Three independent checks:

**(a) The id decodes to the session start time.**

| n_sessions | start_within_5s_of_id | pct | median_offset_s |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13,260 | 11,981 | 90.40 | 1 |

`session` is the epoch-second at which the session began: 90.3% of sessions have their first event within ±5s of it, median offset 1s. It is a real session token, not a row hash.

**(b) It is not globally unique — scope it to the player.**

| n_session_ids | ids_reused_across_players | max_players_per_id |
|---|---|---|
| 13,260 | 26 | 2 |

26 ids collide across exactly 2 players (an epoch-second collision). **Always key on `(PlayerID, session)`.**

**(c) It behaves like a session, not a day bucket.**

| sessions | players | avg_events | median_events | max_events | avg_dur_min | median_dur_min | p90_dur_min | single_event_sessions | sessions_over_12h |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13,286 | 89 | 25 | 7 | 1,542 | 22.20 | 3.70 | 63.50 | 1,628 | 3 |

Median 3.7 min, p90 63.5 min, and only 3 of 13,286 sessions exceed 12h. A day-bucket would have produced ~2,700 sessions of ~24h.


## 2. Cross-check: 30-minute inactivity reconstruction

Inter-event gaps *inside* a native session:

| median_gap_s | p90_s | p99_s | gaps_over_30min | n_gaps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 58 | 1,262.70 | 630 | 318,833 |

Median gap is 2s and only 630 of 318,833 within-session gaps (0.2%) exceed 30 minutes — the native boundaries already respect the inactivity rule.

Sessions rebuilt from scratch with a 30-minute gap:

| sessions | avg_events | median_events | avg_dur_min | median_dur_min | single_event_sessions |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10,432 | 31.80 | 9 | 24.30 | 7.10 | 952 |

Agreement between the two definitions:

| native_sessions | reconstructed_sessions | pct_native_inside_one_reconstructed | avg_native_per_reconstructed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13,286 | 10,432 | 96.90 | 1.33 |

**96.9% of native sessions sit entirely inside a single 30-minute-gap session.** The reconstruction is slightly *coarser* (10,432 vs 13,286) because it merges app restarts and platform switches that happen within 30 minutes.

**Decision: use the native `session` id.** It is real, it agrees with the heuristic, and it correctly splits a player who reopens the app after 5 minutes. The 30-minute rule is kept in `CONFIG['session_gap_minutes']` as a validated fallback for any feed that lacks a session token.

### The headline: the final step

| sessions_added_to_slip | also_submitted | abandoned_slip | final_step_dropoff_pct |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1,649 | 1,232 | 417 | 25.29 |


> **25.29% of sessions that build a betslip never submit it.** 417 of 1,649 sessions reach the slip and abandon it.


**Overall session conversion rate: 9.85%** (1,309 of 13,286 sessions submit a slip); **9.80%** end in an accepted bet. Of sessions that reach *any* intent (add-to-slip or game launch), 31.1% convert.


Funnel is **not strictly nested** — quantified:

| submitted_without_add_event | submitted_total | pct |
|---|---|---|
| 77 | 1,309 | 5.90 |

5.9% of submitted slips have no `betslip_add_bet` in the same session. These are re-bets from `betslip_history` / `betslip-history-overview` / `afterbet` (visible in `added_from`). Re-bet is a *different* journey and should be modelled separately, not treated as leakage.

## 10. Top 5 observations

1. **A real session id already exists** and survives validation — 90.3% of ids decode to their own start time, and 96.9% agree with a 30-minute inactivity rebuild. No sessionisation work is needed; key on `(PlayerID, session)`.
2. **The drop-off is concentrated at the last step.** 1 in 4 sessions that build a betslip (25.29%) never submit it. That is a single, well-defined, addressable moment — not diffuse browsing apathy.
3. **Converting sessions are ~17x more event-dense** (131.6 vs 7.8 events) and 60x longer than browse-only sessions. Session quality is separable early, which makes live scoring realistic.
4. **Intent is fast and shallow-lived**: median 51s to first action, 7.2 min to bet. Any intervention must fire inside the session, in the first minute or two — a next-day email is structurally too late.
5. **Behaviour and money are in separate silos with no key between them.** The event log has sessions but no stake; `SB_Player` has stake but no session and only date-level time. Every euro figure we quote is a modelled estimate — say so on the slide.

## 11. Caveats to keep on the record

- 89 players, all heavy users, one brand (`hr`), one month. Upper-bound rates.
- `betslip_placed_bet` is per-leg, not per-slip (6.8x inflation if misused).
- Platform-specific event vocabularies; `web`/`GM` mix casino and sportsbook on one platform label.
- 5.9% of conversions are re-bets with no in-session add event.
- Abandonment *reasons* are not in the data at all (see DATA_MAP §6).
